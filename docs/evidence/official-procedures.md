### 1. Entry permit and student residence visa
A student may be sponsored either by a UAE-resident parent or by the accredited university or college [1]. The official page requires a certificate from the university that states the duration of study, and it gives no fee and no validity period [2]. The ADU form (2018) lists for an international student a clear color passport copy (passport valid for at least 6 months), one white-background photo, and a "To Whom It May Concern" letter from Admission; the Arabic letter from the registrar's office applies to holders of cancelled or visit visas, not to new international students [8]. Fees on that form are visa process AED 850 (AED 1,400 for holders of cancelled or visit/tourist visas), health insurance AED 800, and visa security deposit AED 5,560 [8]. ADU conditions on the same form are full-time status (12 credit hours undergraduate, 6 postgraduate), a one-month process, application one month before the current visa expires for students already in the UAE, and submission of the medical result and Emirates ID application to the Student Support Service Office within 5 days of obtaining the visa [9].

### 2. Medical fitness test and Emirates ID
Residence visa applicants aged 18 and above must take a medical test, pass a security check, and apply for an Emirates ID [3]. Screening covers HIV/AIDS, hepatitis B, tuberculosis, syphilis, and leprosy, and residency is denied or not renewed for positive HIV/AIDS and leprosy cases [5]. The Emirates ID is mandatory for all citizens and residents; the application goes through the ICP website or an accredited typing centre, and applicants aged 15 and above complete fingerprint and signature procedures at an ICP service centre [6]. Renewal later than one month after expiry costs AED 20 per day up to AED 1,000 [7]. A resident who lives outside the UAE for more than 180 days continuously loses the visa automatically, subject to listed exceptions [4].

### 3. Health insurance
Abu Dhabi health insurance is governed by Health Insurance Law No. 23 of 2005, and the Department of Health states a fine of AED 300 per month for every uninsured month [11]. The ADU form (2018) charges AED 800 for student health insurance [8]. No opened official page states who must provide the cover, so the app should not assert it.

### 4. UAE Pass
UAE Pass is the national digital identity and signature solution; registration uses facial recognition and takes less than 5 minutes without a visit to a service centre, and the app stores, shares, and signs documents; the help desk number is 600 561 111 [13]. The page does not state that an Emirates ID is required, so the app should not assert it.

### 5. SIM card
e& and du are licensed operators, and the Hesabati feature shows every mobile and fixed-line number registered under an Emirates ID [16]. e& requires an original valid Emirates ID from residents and offers visitors a line valid for 90 days; the du rule (UAE ID, or foreign passport with valid UAE visa or residency) comes from a 2012 press release and is dated [17]. No verified source for bank account documents is available yet.

### 6. Transport and airport
The Hafilat card costs AED 10 and is valid for five years; passes cost AED 30 weekly, AED 80 monthly, and AED 500 annually; a local trip costs AED 2, a regional trip AED 2 plus AED 0.05 per km, and an inter-city trip AED 10 plus AED 0.1 per km [19]. Abu Dhabi Link is an app-booked on-demand bus that costs AED 2 in Al Shahama and runs from 6 am to 11 pm [20]. A regular taxi starts at AED 12 in the daytime and is booked through the Abu Dhabi Taxi app or on 600 53 53 53 [21]. From Zayed International Airport, routes A1 (Al Zahiyah) and A2 (Khalifa Street) run 24/7 for about AED 4, payable by Hafilat card only; the card is sold at ticket machines near the airport bus stops [22].

### 7. Off-campus housing
In Abu Dhabi, landlords register the lease in Tawtheeq; a municipality fee of 5% of the rental value or rental index, whichever is higher, is then added to the tenant's water and electricity bill across 12 months [15]. The TAMM registration fee and document list could not be verified from an official page.

### 8. Attestation
Certificates issued abroad are authenticated first by the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in the issuing country and then by the UAE embassy or consulate there [23]. The UAE Ministry of Foreign Affairs runs the attestation service through its website and the UAE MOFA smart application, requires the original document without lamination, handles attestation abroad through selected UAE Missions, and keeps fees in a separate Attestation Guide; the support line is +971 800 44444 [24]. The Ministry of Higher Education and Scientific Research issues the qualification recognition report, which serves as an official document for employment, further studies, visas, and government services [25].

### 9. Part-time work
u.ae lists a student training and employment permit, valid for three months, and a part-time work permit; the establishment applies, not the student [26]. The MoHRE service page lists the documents: photo, Emirates ID copy, proof of student status, valid residence visa for non-national students, guardian consent for ages 15 to 18, medical fitness certificate, a no-objection certificate from the educational institution for training, and an approved contract; the federal fee is AED 50 [27]. Working without a valid MoHRE permit is illegal under Article 6 of Federal Decree-Law No. 33 of 2021 [27].

### 10. Golden visa for outstanding students
Sources differ. u.ae says 5 years for high school achievers and 10 years for top university students, with no GPA thresholds [28]. The ICP guide says 10 years for top secondary school students (minimum 95%) and for UAE university graduates (GPA 3.5 from a Category A university or 3.8 from a Category B university), within two years of graduation and with Ministry of Education attestation; international graduates need a 3.5 GPA from a top-100 university, and the guide states no explicit duration for them [29]. The app should cite ICP and flag the difference. Dubai's GDRFA lists AED 1,100 plus itemised charges of AED 10, AED 10, AED 500 (inside the country), and AED 20, valid 10 years; this applies to Dubai only [30].
